Query

Explain how a home solar energy system works, specifically focusing on how it stores excess energy for later use.

Context

CHUNK

Lithium-ion batteries are widely used in electric vehicles due to their high energy density and long cycle life.

CHUNK

These batteries require careful management to avoid deep discharges, which can reduce lifespan.

Response

A home solar energy system works by using solar panels that capture sunlight through the photovoltaic effect, where photons knock electrons loose to generate electricity. The energy output from the panels can vary depending on factors like orientation, shading, and weather conditions. To store excess energy for later use, systems often rely on lithium-ion batteries, which are known for their high energy density and long life. These batteries can hold electricity produced during sunny periods and release it during times of low sunlight, like at night or on cloudy days. Proper management of battery charging and discharging is important to ensure a long battery lifespan
